# Payment issues

If a payment failed, check that your card details are up to date and that your bank allows online payments, then try again.

Seeing two charges for one purchase is often a temporary authorization hold from your bank. Such holds usually disappear within 3–5 business days. If both charges are still there after that, contact us with the dates and amounts and we will investigate.

If you see a charge you do not recognise, contact us before disputing it with your bank so we can check it quickly.
