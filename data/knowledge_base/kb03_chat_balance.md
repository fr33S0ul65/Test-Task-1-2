# Chat balance and minutes

Chat balance is used for paid live chats with advisors. Minutes are deducted only while a session with an advisor is active.

If you topped up your balance but it was not credited, please send us the payment receipt. After checking the transaction, our team credits the missing amount to your balance.

If a chat session failed to connect but minutes were deducted, our team reviews the session logs and returns the deducted minutes to your balance when the failure is confirmed.

Unused balance does not expire.
