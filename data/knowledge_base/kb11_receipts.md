# Receipts and invoices

For purchases made on the website, a receipt is sent to the email linked to your MyTestBase account right after payment. You can also download past receipts in Profile → Payment history.

For purchases made through the App Store or Google Play, receipts are issued by Apple or Google. You can find them in your Apple ID purchase history or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

We cannot change the name or tax details on a receipt after the payment has been made.
