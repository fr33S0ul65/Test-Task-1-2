# Account deletion and personal data

You can delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete account. Deletion is permanent: your readings, chat history and remaining chat balance cannot be restored.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in the store first.

To request a copy of your personal data or its deletion through support, contact us from the email linked to your MyTestBase account. We verify your identity before processing the request and complete it within 30 days.
