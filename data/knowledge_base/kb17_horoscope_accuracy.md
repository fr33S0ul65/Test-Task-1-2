# How horoscopes and readings are created

Daily horoscopes in MyTestBase are generated from your birth data (date, time and place of birth) and current astrological positions. The more precise your birth time and place, the more personalised your horoscope.

If your horoscope seems inaccurate, first check your birth details in Profile → Edit profile.

Horoscopes and readings are for entertainment and self-reflection and do not predict future events with certainty. We welcome feedback about content quality through Profile → Send feedback.
