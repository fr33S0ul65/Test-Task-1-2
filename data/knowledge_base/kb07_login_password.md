# Login and password reset

To reset your password, tap "Forgot password" on the login screen and enter the email linked to your MyTestBase account. The reset email usually arrives within a few minutes; please check your spam or promotions folder.

If you originally signed up with Apple or Google, use the same "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google" button instead of a password.

If you no longer have access to your email, contact support with your account name and the approximate date of your last purchase so we can verify your identity.

If you notice logins or purchases you did not make, contact support immediately so we can secure your account.
