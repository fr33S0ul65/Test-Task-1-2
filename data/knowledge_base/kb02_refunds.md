# Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed individually by our billing team, usually within 3 business days. A refund is never issued automatically.

We usually approve refunds when:
- you were charged twice for the same purchase;
- you were charged after cancelling your subscription;
- a paid session could not take place because of a technical problem on our side.

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by Apple or Google under their own rules. In that case we explain how to request the refund from them.

To speed up the review, please share the date and amount of the charge and the email linked to your MyTestBase account.
