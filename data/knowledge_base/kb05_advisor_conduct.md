# Advisor conduct and session quality

All MyTestBase advisors must be respectful and professional. Advisors must never ask you to pay outside the MyTestBase app or share personal contact details.

You can rate every session and report an advisor from the chat history. Reports are reviewed by our Advisor Quality team, which may contact you for details.

If an advisor ended a session early or did not respond, the unused minutes are reviewed and returned to your balance when the report is confirmed.

Readings reflect the advisor's interpretation and are not a guarantee of future events.
