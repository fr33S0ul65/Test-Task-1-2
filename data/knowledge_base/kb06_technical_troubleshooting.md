# Technical troubleshooting

If the app crashes or does not load:
1. Make sure you have the latest version of MyTestBase from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Restart your device.
3. If the problem continues, delete and reinstall the app. Your account and purchases are kept.

If a chat with an advisor is stuck on "connecting", check your internet connection and try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

When reporting a bug, please include your device model, operating system version, app version and a short description or screenshot of the problem.
