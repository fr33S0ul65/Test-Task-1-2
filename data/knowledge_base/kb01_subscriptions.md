# Subscriptions: cancellation, trial and renewal

You can cancel your MyTestBase subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; you keep premium access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

How to cancel depends on where you subscribed:
- iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MyTestBase → Cancel Subscription.
- Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → MyTestBase → Cancel.
- Website: log in at mytestbase.example → Profile → Manage subscription → Cancel.

Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

Free trials convert to a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. You can see your next renewal date on the Manage subscription page.
